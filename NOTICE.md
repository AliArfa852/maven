## What was modified

- Product renamed from Odysseus to Maven, with corresponding rebranding throughout the user-facing documentation and interface.
- Environment variables aliased: `MAVEN_AI_*` is the new prefix; legacy `ODYSSEUS_*` environment variables remain functional and are automatically mapped.
- Command-line scripts renamed: `scripts/maven` and `scripts/maven-<x>` are the new entry points; legacy `scripts/odysseus-*` names remain functional as thin shims.
